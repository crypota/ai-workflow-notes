# Output review

## Technical

- [ ] Files open in the intended application.
- [ ] Required schema, dimensions or output format match the specification.
- [ ] Relevant commands or validations were actually run and results saved.
- [ ] Missing assets, dependencies and unsupported steps are documented.

## Factual

- [ ] Each material factual claim has evidence or is labeled unverified.
- [ ] Dates, units, names and identifiers match the sources.
- [ ] Inference is distinguished from observation.
- [ ] Negative results and contradictory evidence are retained.

## Visual (when applicable)

- [ ] Output was inspected at final display size, not only as a thumbnail.
- [ ] Identity, silhouette, proportions, colors and required accessories match references.
- [ ] Occluded/reconstructed regions are explicitly identified.
- [ ] Cropping, transparency, alignment and readable text were checked.

## Publication

- [ ] Public artifacts contain no credentials, private inputs or internal URLs.
- [ ] Examples are labeled as real, synthetic or illustrative.
- [ ] Claims of testing reflect completed checks, with known limitations.
- [ ] Another person can follow the instructions and locate the evidence.
